    %% ==================== 登入流程 ====================

    SetupQpay --> DetermineView
    SetupFee --> DetermineView

    LoadPersonalData --> ShowPersonalData[顯示個人資料]

    NewPersonChoice -->|查看名單| ViewNewPersonList[GetNewPersonList<br/>查看新人名單]

    NewPersonValidCheck -->|失敗| NewPersonError[顯示驗證錯誤]
    NewPersonError --> InputNewPersonData
    
    NewPersonValidCheck -->|成功| SaveNewPerson[SaveToDatabase<br/>儲存至資料庫]
    SaveNewPerson --> SaveNewPersonCheck{儲存成功?}
    
    SaveNewPersonCheck -->|成功| NewPersonSuccess[新人建立成功]
    SaveNewPersonCheck -->|失敗| NewPersonSaveError[儲存失敗]
    
    NewPersonSuccess --> MainMenu
    NewPersonSaveError --> InputNewPersonData

    ShowNewPersonList --> MainMenu
    
    UpdateNewPerson --> SelectNewPerson[選擇要更新的新人]

    EditNewPersonData --> SaveNewPerson
    
    DeleteNewPerson --> ConfirmDelete{確認刪除?}
    ConfirmDelete -->|取消| MainMenu
    ConfirmDelete -->|確定| ExecuteDelete[執行刪除]
    ExecuteDelete --> MainMenu

    EquipmentMenu --> EquipmentChoice{設備管理功能}
    
    EquipmentChoice -->|設備清單| EquipmentView[EquipmentView<br/>設備清單]
    EquipmentChoice -->|聯絡資訊| EquipmentContact[EquipmentContactView<br/>設備聯絡人]
    EquipmentChoice -->|課程存檔| EquipmentLesson[EquipmentStorLessonsView<br/>課程存檔]

    MyPayFlow --> MyPayLogin[MyPay 登入頁面]

    ValidateMyPayData --> MyPayValidCheck{驗證通過?}
